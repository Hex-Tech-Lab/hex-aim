### Horizon-by-Horizon Comparison

Across the successive wave requests in this session, the generation strategy underwent a clear three-stage evolution:

#### 1. Horizons 1 & 2 (Waves 19 – 28): Functional Engine Bootstrap
- **Primary Driver**: Bottom-up pipeline construction and core functional mechanics.
- **Scope**: Focused on solving acute operational needs (e.g., Telegram photo intake, OCR ingestion, visual scope-ring tokens, initial exam runner, basic MIS export, and privacy vault encryption).
- **Thinking Characteristics**: Linear feature planning, direct response to platform gaps, and standard software component design.

#### 2. Horizon 3 (Waves 29 – 33): Cognitive Modeling & Pedagogical Closed-Loop
- **Primary Driver**: Scientific cognitive architecture and end-to-end learning loops.
- **Scope**: Introduced formal ADRs (ADR-005), mathematical retention decay ($R(t) = R_0 \cdot 2^{-t/S}$), graduated anxiety curves (Bloom 1–4), screen-free Socratic voice retrieval, anti-clustered homework scheduling, and $\epsilon$-differential privacy.
- **Thinking Characteristics**: Shifted from building features to modeling the child's mind, memory decay, and parent-teacher institutional alignment.

#### 3. Horizon 4 (Waves 34 – 38): Venture Moats, Somatic Telemetry & Statutory Governance
- **Primary Driver**: Helmer’s *7 Powers*, physiological sensory regulation, and legal/statutory lock-in.
- **Scope**: Expanded into fine-motor dysgraphia ink telemetry, local-first CRDT offline resilience, automated UK SEND Section F statutory reviews, real-time procedural Web Audio binaural soundscapes, and asynchronous 60-second parent micro-podcasts.
- **Thinking Characteristics**: Evaluated every wave across 26 venture and systems dimensions, explicitly engineering high barriers to entry, counter-positioning against incumbents, and compounding data flywheels.
