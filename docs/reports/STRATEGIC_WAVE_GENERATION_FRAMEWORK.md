### Core Analytical Paradigm

The 5-wave strategic trajectories were synthesized by applying a three-tier cognitive and venture deduction model:

1. **First-Principles Domain Triad**:
   - Every wave resolves the tension between three competing constraints: child neurodevelopmental reality (ADHD working memory limits and Ebbinghaus cognitive decay), statutory curriculum standards (UK Key Stage 2 and GL Assessment rubrics), and household operational friction (parent time poverty and homework anxiety).

2. **Sequential Defensibility Escalation**:
   - Waves follow an intentional compounding architecture:
     - *Phase 1 (Verification & Auditing)*: Establishing immutable truth and cryptographic audit trails.
     - *Phase 2 (Modal Expansion)*: Removing screen barriers via voice and multi-modal sensory channels.
     - *Phase 3 (Generative Closed-Loop)*: Translating diagnosed gaps into personalized, anxiety-regulated exam synthesis.
     - *Phase 4 (Behavioral Habituation)*: Anchoring routines directly into family calendars and native messaging apps.
     - *Phase 5 (Network Capture)*: Unlocking institutional data network effects through privacy-preserving federated exchange.

3. **Inversion & Friction Minimization**:
   - Each wave identifies the primary failure point of existing EdTech incumbents (e.g., test anxiety freeze, Wi-Fi drops, parental interrogation friction) and architects an unfair advantage by engineering the direct structural counter-measure.
